# Brand Marketing and Guidelines

## 1. Intro

&emsp;&emsp;Please be sure to read these guidelines first before using any brand assets. 

&emsp;&emsp;Although zerrow distributes some of its technology under open-source licenses, the zerrow brands are not distributed under the same licenses. 

&emsp;&emsp;The following zerrow "Brand Assets" are copyrighted material, and you may only use them in accordance with the policy detailed below: 

&emsp;&emsp;The zerrow logos, word mark, icon 

&emsp;&emsp;The zerrow name

&emsp;&emsp;Other visual assets produced by zerrow

## 2. What you may do

&emsp;&emsp;You may use zerrow's Brand Assets when you are: 

&emsp;&emsp;Referring to our products and services 

&emsp;&emsp;Linking to our official site or products, such as "Lending on zerrow"

&emsp;&emsp;Announcing an official partnership, but only if you have official approval by a qualified member of the zerrow team 

&emsp;&emsp;For information on other uses of our Brand Assets, please reach out at 

## 3. What you may not do

&emsp;&emsp;We firmly believe in the open-source ethos of Web3 but protecting the zerrow brand is extremely important for our users' security, so that users know what a genuine product/endorsement is and what isn't. 

&emsp;&emsp;Here are a few examples of forbidden uses of our Brand Assets: 

&emsp;&emsp;Don't use our Brand Assets in your products' name, logo, NFTs, etc.  

&emsp;&emsp;For example, making an app called "ZERROW CAR" or "ZERROW BIKE" are not allowed.

&emsp;&emsp;Don't use the zerrow logo in whole or in part as part of your products' logos, marketing material, website, or other. 

&emsp;&emsp;Logos with a strong resemblance to the zerrow Icon are also forbidden. 

&emsp;&emsp;Don't use our Brand Assets to create digital or physical products for sale, including but not limited to:
&emsp;&emsp; Merchandise ("Swag")   NFTs   Other products 

&emsp;&emsp;Don't create derivative names that imply official endorsement 

&emsp;&emsp;For example, don't make a social media group called " zerrow Spain" - this sounds official. " zerrow Fans of Spain" may be permitted.

&emsp;&emsp;Don't change or adapt our Brand Assets in any way 

&emsp;&emsp;Use the logos and other assets exactly as provided. Do not change the colors, reassemble, abbreviate, or change these assets in any way.
